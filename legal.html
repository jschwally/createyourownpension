Texas Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association

Insurance policies issued by insurance companies doing business in Texas are subject to the protections of the Texas Life and Health Insurance Guaranty Association, or TLHIGA, up to certain limits in the event a member insurance company is found to be insolvent and ordered into liquidation by a court. TLHIGA is a nonprofit, industry funded safety net, not a government agency, and its protections are not equivalent to FDIC deposit insurance or any other government guarantee. Coverage limits and eligibility requirements apply and vary by policy type. TLHIGA protection should not be used as the primary basis for selecting an insurance product or insurance company. Evaluating the financial strength and claims paying ability of any insurance company before purchase remains an important part of the due diligence process. For more information visit www.txlifega.org.
